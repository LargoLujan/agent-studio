"Work" shows you how the project is going: its goals, what is pending and who did each thing. Everything is read from the verified history.

## What it does
- A milestone is a named goal. It opens with requirements from the backlog, which are frozen, and it closes with an audit.
- The board puts each requirement in its column. None reaches "Done" without an approved verdict.
- The chain joins a requirement to its delivery. From a node you can open a saved test run or a verdict.
- Participants says who took each role. If no source says so, it reads "No consta".
- The links the system infers count only when you confirm them.
- A budget limits tokens or runs. The engine does not launch an attempt that would pass it.

## What it does not do
It does not move cards by hand or invent what is not recorded. Closing a milestone asks your approval and uses the usage limit of your plan.

## Tips
- Note the requirements in the backlog before you open the milestone: they cannot be changed once it is open.
- If you close a milestone and something is missing, you can reopen it. The previous report is kept.

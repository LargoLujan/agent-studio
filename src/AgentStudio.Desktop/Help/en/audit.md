The audit reviews the whole project and tells you what it finds, without changing anything.

## What it does
- It reads the state of the project and what the log remembers of the work done.
- An independent auditor gives its opinion and leaves it in writing.

## What it does not do
It does not modify your files. What comes out is a report: you decide what to do with it.

## Tips
- Audit when you close an important stage.
- If the report points out a problem, you can open a debate to decide how to fix it.

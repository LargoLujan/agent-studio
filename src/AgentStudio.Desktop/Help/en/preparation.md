Here you get everything ready before asking the agents for anything.

## What you do here
- **Write what you want** as requirements. When they are right, you freeze them: from then on they do not change by surprise.
- **Look at the team**: which agents there are and what each one can do.
- **Choose how the work is checked** (for example, which tests run).
- **Talk to the orchestrator** if you prefer to explain your idea in your own words. Each message uses a little of the usage limit of your plan and asks for your OK.
- **Approve the plan.** Nothing starts without your approval.

## Tips
- One requirement, one idea. If it has two, split it in two.
- Before approving, look at the estimate: it tells you how much the plan may spend.
- If something is not clear, the analysis panel warns you about what is missing.

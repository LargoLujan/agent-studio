Auto Doctor checks whether your computer is ready to work on the project. It runs nothing of the project.

## What it checks
- The agent programs, their version and whether you are signed in.
- The tools of your computer, such as .NET, Node, Python and Git.
- What the project says it needs, against what you have installed.
- Docker and WSL, only when the project uses them.

## What it does with what it finds
Each check ends as correct, warning, error or not checked. Not checked is not correct: it means nobody has looked at it.

## The fixes
A fix takes you to a screen, explains what to do, or changes something of Agent Studio that you can undo later. It never installs anything on your computer.

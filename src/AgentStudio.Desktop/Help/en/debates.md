A debate helps you decide something hard with more than one point of view.

## How it goes
1. You write the question and the criteria.
2. Two agents defend different positions.
3. A judge from another company reads it all and gives a verdict.

## The verdict
It is a card with the decision, the reasons and what is left unresolved. If you disagree, you can **challenge it**: the debate reopens with your objection.

## Tips
- A clear question and concrete criteria make better debates.
- A debate uses the quota of several agents: look at the estimate first.
